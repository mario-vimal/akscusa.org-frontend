Public conference call with **Comrade Kausalya Sankar**, social activist, and **Comrade Suganthi**, Secretary, All India Democratic Women's Association (AIDWA), Tamil Nadu.

On March 13, 2016, Kausalya's husband V. Shankar, a Dalit, was brutally murdered by caste fanatics at Udumalpet in Tamil Nadu, India. Kausalya boldly fought for justice for her husband. Comrade Suganthi has participated in and led several campaigns against caste discrimination and gender violence.
