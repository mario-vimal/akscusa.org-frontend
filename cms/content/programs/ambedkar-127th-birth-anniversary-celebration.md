Dr. Ambedkar's 127th Birthday Celebration - **Social Justice in the Neo-Liberal Era**.

Speakers from various organizations presented their views on how social justice is being denied in the current economic situation. The audience participated in an open forum question-and-answer session, which was the best part of all, with the right questions and forceful answers. AKSC joined hands with many organizations to pull this program together at Stanford University.
