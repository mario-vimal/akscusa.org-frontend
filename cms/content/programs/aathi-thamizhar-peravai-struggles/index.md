Public conference call with **Comrade Athiyamaan**, founder of Aathi Thamizhar Peravai (ATP).

ATP is an "Arunthathiyar" (Dalit) movement founded by Athiyamaan in 1994 at Coimbatore district of Tamil Nadu, India. Its goals are to re-establish economic, cultural and social status among the Arunthathiyar community, who are considered outcast, oppressed by all other castes, non-caste societies and whose living condition is below the poverty line.

Comrade Athiyamaan also inaugurated the AKSC website during this call.
