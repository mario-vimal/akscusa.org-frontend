Join us to celebrate the 147th birth anniversary of Thanthai Periyar, the pioneer of Self-Respect, Rationalism, Social Justice, Gender Equality and Human Rights.

## ★ Listen and learn from our special speakers

### Sharika Thiranagama

Associate Professor, Stanford University

**The concept of Respect: A meditation on Self-Respect**

### Christelle Mombo-Zigah

Author | Global Business Strategist

**Fair, Authentic & Accurate Representations in Today's Digital World**

## Share your talents

This event included a program for people to express Periyar's ideals through:

- **Speech**
- **Music**
- **Songs**
- **Poetry**
- **Art**
- **Essay**

**Got talents?** Participation registration was offered at [bit.ly/periyar2025sf](https://bit.ly/periyar2025sf).

General audience members did not need to register; they could simply join the celebration.

**Date:** Saturday, September 13, 2025, from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM

**Venue:** Irvington Community Center, 41885 Blacow Road, Fremont, California

**Registration:** [bit.ly/periyar2025sf](https://bit.ly/periyar2025sf) (only to showcase your talents)

Let us come together to honor Periyar's legacy and inspire new generations through creativity and collective spirit.

All are welcome!
