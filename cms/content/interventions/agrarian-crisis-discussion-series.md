Over **200,000,000** Indians sleep hungry every night which includes farmers, agricultural workers, industrial & unorganized workers and socially marginalized and **23·2%** of men aged between 15 and 54 and **53·2%** of women aged between 15 and 49 are found anemic when India let 65 lakh tonnes of grain go to waste in four months, Why?

In July 2019, the RSS/BJP administration promulgated **three ordinances** and passed them as law in Sep 2020. What are those laws? Why does the RSS/BJP administration push such laws in the midst of the COVID19 pandemic?

How the small scale protests started in **Aug 2020** grew in large numbers in **Nov 2020**? Between Nov 28 and Dec 3, close to 300,000 farmers arrived in Delhi. How is growing as a pan India protest?

What **lies** are the RSS/BJP administration is spreading through their huge propaganda machinery about **MSP**(Minimum Support Price) and **APMC**(Agricultural Produce Market Committee)?

We need to understand these questions not only to save the farmers and Indian agriculture but also importantly how these bills will kill **the food security** of the common people which leaves us to the mercy of corporates for the food.

Please join **the education series on peasant questions** in general and current **peasant’s protest** in particular.
