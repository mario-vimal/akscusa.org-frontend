## Introduction

The **State of California Department of Fair Employment and Housing**(DFEH)’s lawsuit against **Cisco Systems** for caste discrimination recognizes a long-hidden malaise in the Indian diaspora. Even after complaints by the victim in November 2016 to the Cisco HR about ‘the discriminatory behavior’ of his so-called upper caste managers and subsequent inquiry and discussions, Cisco failed to take action, arguing that ‘_caste discrimination is not unlawful_‘. It is clear that caste conceited Indians who practice caste in their day-to-day life – having learned and brought it to America – carefully conceal their practices of caste discrimination and bigotry. Such devious concealment is accomplished partly by portraying Indians in the USA as a monolithic entity. It also shows that American corporates are unaware of the endemic practice of caste among Indians – in India and the diaspora.

The aim of this series is to encourage anti-caste Indians to openly discuss the practice of caste in the USA and to **_educate the broader American public about the caste system and the discriminatory practices that surreptitiously continue,_**  especially among those working in Science, Technology, Engineering and Math (STEM).
