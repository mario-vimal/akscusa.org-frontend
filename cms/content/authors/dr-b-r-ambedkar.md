bio: |-
  Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar (1891–1956) was a jurist, economist, and the leader of the movement of India's Dalits against caste. He chaired the drafting committee of the Constitution of India and served as independent India's first law minister, resigning in 1951 when the Hindu Code Bill was defeated.

  He wrote across law, economics, history, and religion, and the circle has worked through several of those books. In October 1956, weeks before his death, he converted to Buddhism at Nagpur along with hundreds of thousands of followers.
