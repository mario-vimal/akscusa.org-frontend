  Manoj Mitta is an Indian journalist who has reported on law, civil rights, and communal violence for three decades, at the Indian Express, India Today, and the Times of India.

  He is the co-author, with H. S. Phoolka, of When a Tree Shook Delhi, on the 1984 massacre of Sikhs and the impunity that followed it, and the author of Caste Pride, a legal history of the battles for equality fought in Hindu India since the nineteenth century.
