bio: |-
  Kancha Ilaiah Shepherd (born 1952) is an Indian political theorist and writer on caste, and a former head of the Centre for the Study of Social Exclusion and Inclusive Policy at Maulana Azad National Urdu University in Hyderabad.

  He writes on the productive knowledge of the Shudra, Dalit, and Adivasi castes — the work of herding, tilling, tanning, and weaving — and on the Hindu order that lives on that work while holding it in contempt. He has published under both Kancha Ilaiah and Kancha Ilaiah Shepherd, which is why this entry carries both names.
