bio: |-
  Gail Omvedt (1941–2021) was a sociologist and writer on caste, class, and the anti-caste movement in India. Born in Minneapolis, she came to India for her doctoral research on the non-Brahmin movement in western India, settled in Kasegaon in Maharashtra, and took Indian citizenship in 1983.

  She wrote on Phule and on Ambedkar, on Dalit and non-Brahmin politics, and on the peasant and women's movements she took part in alongside her research rather than only wrote about.
