![Promotional poster for the 2025 AKSC Annual Conference, titled “Forging Indian Diasporic Solidarity: Confronting Oppression Within,” with the date and Los Cerritos Community Center venue.](/media/conferences/aksc-annual-conference-2025-poster.jpg)

For many, moving abroad promises freedom from the caste system. The reality, however, is that caste follows them, showing up in community gatherings, WhatsApp groups, on college campuses, and in professional settings.

Casteist discussions go unchallenged in workplaces. Oppressed-caste employees and anti-caste allies remain silent out of fear of retaliation. Anti-caste policies are vague. Since caste is disguised as “culture” or treated as a private matter, criticism of caste is framed as an attack on individual freedom. There are no social or legal protections for the caste oppressed or their anti-caste allies.

If anybody dares to voice out, those with caste privilege use power and money to silence the truth. We saw it in the fight over SB 403 in California, where protections for the caste-oppressed were blocked under pressure from wealthy donors. These battles make two things clear: that legal protection is absolutely essential for the caste-oppressed; and that the grave silence around caste oppression needs to be effectively challenged by all.

**_However, are conditions viable today, for anti-caste speech and action to emerge in mainstream spaces?_**

Join us in our annual conference to deliberate on how to create the conditions necessary to empower people to break the silence on the topic of caste. To have open and frank discussions on how to create social and legal protections against caste exclusion and oppression. Each one of us has a unique role in forging solidarity by confronting exclusion within. We can make an anti-caste future actually and truly possible, here and everywhere.

Also join us in celebration of legal victories on anti-caste litigations in the USA.

## Poster session call

AKSC invited submissions for a poster session centred around the conference theme: **Forging Indian Diasporic Solidarity: Confronting Oppression Within**.

The session offered an opportunity to share research, community projects, organizing strategies, or creative interventions that spoke to the shared themes of resisting global oppression, confronting caste in India and abroad, and building solidarities across movements.

Participants were encouraged to present work-in-progress, hypotheses and/or results relating to data-driven or qualitative research, or visual storytelling. Posters could take academic, activist, or artistic forms. AKSC offered to print posters free of charge for participants without access to printing.

Submissions included a poster title and 150–200 word abstract, plus the presenter's name, affiliation (if any), and contact information. The deadline was **11:59 PM PST on September 28, 2025**.
