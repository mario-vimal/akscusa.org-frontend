There’s no single “right” response to casteist remarks — context and power
dynamics matter. This playbook will offer practical, contextual responses you
can use with confidence.

**We need your input to build it well.**

Explore the illustrated real-life scenarios below — moments you might
recognize. Tell us what you’ve experienced, what you wish you’d said, or what an
ally could have said, using the
[Anticaste Toolkit form](https://forms.gle/KseyjgpuD9Q8NaCn8).
