The comics on this page were drawn to be read together, out loud, with someone
who does not already agree with you. They are shared for that purpose: read them
in a classroom, a union meeting, a reading circle, or at home.

Every panel carries a **transcript** of the words drawn inside it, so the comics
can be read by a screen reader, quoted, and translated. Any panel can be opened
full screen, and once one is open the arrow keys move through the rest.
